## The Watchtower Brand

The "Watchtower" name, logo, and associated visual identity are trademarks of Keshav K. The MIT license covers the source code, not the brand.

## What You Can Do

- Use "Watchtower" to refer to this project in articles, blog posts, and discussions
- Say your project is "compatible with Watchtower" or "built for Watchtower"
- Use the name in community contexts when referring to the official project

## What You Cannot Do

- Use "Watchtower" as the name of a fork or derivative work
- Use the Watchtower logo in your own project, product, or service
- Imply that your project is endorsed by, affiliated with, or an official part of Watchtower
- Register domain names or social media accounts using "Watchtower"

## Forks

If you fork Watchtower, you must:

1. Choose a different name for your fork
2. Remove the Watchtower logo and branding
3. Clearly state that your project is a fork and not the official Watchtower
